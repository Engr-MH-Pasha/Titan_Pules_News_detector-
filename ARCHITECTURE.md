# ⚡ Titan Pulse: System Architecture

## 1. Executive Summary
**Titan Pulse** is an automated intelligence aggregation and short-form content production pipeline designed to monitor the world's most influential figures across technology, finance, and global power. The system extracts breaking headlines, runs algorithmic credibility and monetization compliance checks, and generates high-retention 40-second video production packages optimized for Facebook Reels targeting Tier-1 audiences (US, UK, and Australia).
